Update Stock Data via URL

Use this format in your URL:

https://pkjmesra.github.io/PKScreener/?symbol=SYMBOL&open=PRICE&high=PRICE&low=PRICE&close=PRICE&volume=NUMBER

For multiple symbols, separate with commas:

https://pkjmesra.github.io/PKScreener/?symbol=RELIANCE,INFY&open=2650,1550&high=2680,1575&low=2645,1545&close=2675,1568&volume=1500000,850000
